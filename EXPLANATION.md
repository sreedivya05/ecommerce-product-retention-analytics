# Project Explanation

## Business problem
Management needs to understand sales performance while avoiding the mistake of judging performance only by revenue.

## Main KPIs
Total Sales = sum of transaction sales after discount.

Profit = Sales - Cost.

Profit Margin = Profit / Sales × 100.

AOV = Sales / Orders.

## Analysis
The project breaks performance down by month, region, category, product and salesperson.

This helps identify cases such as high revenue but weak profitability.

## Interview answer
"I built a sales BI project using transactional, product, customer and salesperson data. I used SQL and Python to calculate revenue, profit, margin and AOV, then analyzed performance by region, product and salesperson. The goal was to identify areas where strong revenue did not necessarily translate into strong profitability."

## Limitations
The dataset is synthetic and does not prove causality. A low-margin region, for example, may require further investigation into discounting, logistics, competition or product mix.

## Future improvements
Forecasting, Pareto analysis, cohort analysis, automated refresh, anomaly detection and Power BI.
